About

Trained at the bench and on the factory floor — now aiming both at graduate research in sustainable food systems.

I am Sindhuja Sivakumar, a Bachelor of Science graduate in Microbiology (2021–2024) from Pondicherry University, India. My academic training and industrial exposure have strengthened my interest in sustainable biotechnology, agricultural microbiology, food microbiology, and food science and nutrition.
